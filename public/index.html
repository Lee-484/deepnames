How to play
  • Split into the Ministry and the Authority. Each side picks one spymaster; everyone else is an operative.
  • 20 words are dealt. Only the spymasters can see which words belong to which side.
  • On your turn, your spymaster gives a one-word clue and a number: how many cards it points to.
  • Operatives tap cards to suggest them, then press Reveal. You can keep guessing while you're right, up to the number plus one.
  • A bystander or the other side's card ends your turn. Revealing the Vantablack Warrior loses the game instantly.
  • First side to find all its agents wins. The side that goes first has 7 agents; the other has 6.
